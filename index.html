Design and Simulation of a High-Gain DC–DC Converter for Electric Vehicle Applications

Completed

Designed and evaluated a high-gain non-isolated DC–DC converter for EV battery charging using MATLAB–SIMULINK. The converter employed a dual-duty-cycle approach to achieve high voltage gain without extreme duty ratios. A state-space model was developed to analyze voltage gain and stability, and performance was evaluated under varying input voltages and switching frequencies with practical loss considerations.

State of Charge Estimation of Li-ion Batteries Using Extended Kalman Filter

Completed

Developed a MATLAB-based Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) to accurately estimate the State of Charge (SOC) of a Li-ion battery for electric vehicle applications. A first-order Thevenin battery model with SOC- and temperature-dependent parameters was implemented, using real current and voltage data. EKF-estimated SOC and terminal voltage closely matched Coulomb Counting results, demonstrating robustness under noisy and dynamic load conditions.

Battery Modeling (Thevenin) Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) MATLAB Data Analysis SOC Estimation Noise & Error Analysis View Details
